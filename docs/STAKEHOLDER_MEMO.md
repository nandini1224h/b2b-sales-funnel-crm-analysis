# Stakeholder memo — simulated B2B funnel review

**Scenario:** written as an analyst reporting to the Head of Sales and Head of Marketing of a fictional B2B SaaS company. This is a practice deliverable based on simulated data.

**Audience:** fictional Head of Sales and Head of Marketing. **Status:** portfolio recommendation; not delivered to actual stakeholders. **Dataset:** engineered synthetic records, January–June 2026 leads, 30 September snapshot.

## Decision requested

Approve the design of a limited, measured acquisition experiment before any permanent channel reallocation. The objective is to improve qualified wins per acquisition rupee while preserving lead volume, sales capacity and customer quality.

## Findings

Across 24,000 mature leads, 2,420 won within 90 days: 10.08% overall conversion. Referrals converted 1,276 of 4,000 leads, or 31.9%, versus 52 of 4,000 outbound leads, or 1.3%. This is a 24.54× rate ratio and a 30.6 percentage-point difference. These values were designed into a synthetic dataset.

Outbound's proposal-to-win rate is approximately 4.61%, compared with 48.46% for referrals. Review qualification criteria, account fit, proposal recipients and pricing objections before concluding that representative execution is the cause.

Rep conversion spans 8.33%–11.67%, a 40% relative difference. Standardizing all reps to the same source distribution reduces the top-to-bottom difference to roughly 35.6%. Remaining variation is descriptive; source adjustment alone cannot identify individual skill.

Referral acquisition spend per win is INR 313.48 and outbound INR 11,538.46 under the fictional spend assumptions. Costs exclude sales salaries and overhead, so these are acquisition-cost proxies. Revenue-to-spend ratios are inflated by simplified synthetic economics and must not be used for financial forecasting.

## Recommended actions

**First, audit the diagnosis.** Review a stratified sample of outbound and referral leads across qualification and proposal outcomes. Use account fit, confirmed buying intent, stakeholder authority, loss notes and timestamp quality as a review checklist. Validate that attribution rules and source costs are comparable. This is proposed work, not completed work in this project.

**Second, run a capped pilot.** As an illustrative plan, move up to INR 100,000 of the INR 600,000 outbound allocation into partner development and organic acquisition for an eight-week acquisition period. Keep prospecting capacity above a pre-agreed strategic-account floor. Do not assume referrals can absorb the entire amount or produce leads on demand.

**Third, improve the process where leakage is concentrated.** Pilot a qualification checklist before proposal creation, standardize mandatory fields and review reasons for unqualified proposals. Coach based on call/CRM evidence and comparable account assignments; avoid ranking reps solely from raw conversion.

## Measurement plan

Record the hypothesis and eligible account definition before launching. If feasible, randomly assign eligible partner or account clusters to new treatment versus business as usual, balancing by region and baseline activity. Use cluster assignment when spillovers within a partner are likely. Analyze at the assignment unit or use appropriate clustered uncertainty estimates. Channel labels themselves are not randomized; comparing referral versus outbound leads after the pilot would still confound source selection with treatment.

Primary outcome: incremental qualified won accounts per acquisition rupee within 90 days, using a fixed lead-created window. Track total wins and total spend separately; a ratio can improve when volume collapses.

Secondary outcomes: lead-to-qualified rate, proposal-to-win rate, sales cycle, booked deal value and source-adjusted rep outcomes. Guardrails: qualified lead volume, sales workload, customer fit, cancellation/refund rate and 90-day contribution margin when cost data becomes available.

Choose sample size using a realistic baseline, minimum worthwhile lift, assignment-unit variation and available partner volume. These are not estimated by the synthetic dataset. Do not assert a specific experiment power or sample size without those inputs.

Run acquisition for eight weeks, then wait for the final lead cohort's 90-day observation period before final win-rate assessment. Use early reviews for data quality, capacity and guardrails, not repeated unadjusted significance testing. Predefine stop rules with stakeholders and record any changes before inspecting treatment effects.

## Decision rule and uncertainty

Expand only if the pilot supports positive incremental contribution after costs, the lower end of a suitable uncertainty interval is acceptable to decision-makers, and volume/capacity/customer-quality guardrails hold. If volume is too low, extend recruitment or keep the decision provisional rather than declaring no effect.

Historical average economics provide prioritization, not causal estimates or marginal scalability. This project proposes an experiment and reporting framework; it does not claim implemented budget changes, realized uplift, management approval or verified company outcomes.

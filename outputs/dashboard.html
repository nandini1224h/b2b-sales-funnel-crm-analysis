Decision brief

Partner referrals convert at 31.9% (1,276/4,000); cold outbound at 1.3% (52/4,000). The ratio is 24.54×, and the absolute gap is 30.6 percentage points. These values were deliberately engineered in a simulated dataset.

Highest rep conversion is 11.67%; lowest is 8.33%. The relative gap is 40%, or 3.33 percentage points. Rep comparisons need source, territory, tenure and account-quality controls before performance action.

Recommend a limited reallocation experiment, with qualified lead volume, contribution margin, capacity and marginal acquisition cost guardrails. No budget was actually moved and no realized uplift is claimed.
